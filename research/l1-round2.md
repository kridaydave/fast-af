## 5. Final verdict

**Paste-ready after edits.**

`research/l1-fix.md` is much better than the current spec L1: it fixes the core mechanism error (step=loop), removes the equal-TPS kill bar, adds hard gates, adds concrete metrics/noise floors, and shrinks the pilot to a free-tier-feasible size.
